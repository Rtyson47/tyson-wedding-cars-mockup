2. How a booking is made

You tell us your date and what you need, and we send you a quote in writing (by email, text or WhatsApp). Your booking is confirmed when we've received your deposit and sent you a written confirmation. Until then, the date isn't held for you.

The confirmation lists the car or cars, the date, the addresses and the times. Please check it carefully and tell us straight away if anything is wrong.

3. What's included in the price

Unless your confirmation says otherwise, the price includes:

  • the car or cars you've booked, with a uniformed chauffeur;
  • ribbons in colours to match your wedding;
  • the journeys and times shown on your confirmation;
  • waiting at the ceremony of up to how long, e.g. 1 hour;
  • time for photographs with the car of up to how long, e.g. 30 minutes.

Our price is based on the car, the distance and the hours needed, so it's quoted for each booking. If you later add journeys, stops or time, we'll tell you the new price before you agree to it.

On the day, extra time is charged at £ per hour, or per part hour, and only if your chauffeur can stay. We'll always ask before running into extra time if we can.

5. Before the day

About two weeks before your wedding, Helen will ring you to go through the times, addresses and any changes. Please tell us as soon as anything changes, especially addresses and timings. We can't be responsible for delays caused by details we weren't given.

If you'd like to change something, such as the times, the journeys or the car, ask us and we'll tell you whether we can and whether the price changes. We'll confirm any change in writing.

Or a charge?. The price may change if the new date is in a busier part of the year. If we can't offer the new date, section 8 applies.

6. Roads, access and weather

Many of our cars are vintage and sit low to the ground. When you book, please tell us about any narrow lanes, unmade tracks, very steep hills or tight gateways on the route. If we think a road isn't safe for your car, we'll tell you before the day and offer to collect or drop off at the nearest safe point, or offer you a different car. If neither suits you, we'll refund what you've paid for that car.

We need somewhere to park safely at each address. If parking costs money, who pays?

Our landaulettes open at the back. Whether the hood goes down is your chauffeur's decision on the day, depending on the weather. In snow, ice, flooding or other severe weather, we may need to use a different car or change the route to keep everyone safe. We'll never drive a car in conditions our chauffeur thinks are unsafe.

Our chauffeur chooses the route. If you'd like a particular route, such as the scenic way round, just ask. If it adds time, it may cost extra.

7. If your car can't run

We look after our cars carefully, but some of them are nearly a hundred years old, and any car can break down. If the car you booked can't run on your day:

  • we'll send another car from our own fleet, as close in style as we can;
  • if the replacement is cheaper, we'll refund the difference, and if it costs more, you won't pay the extra;
  • if none of our own cars is free, we'll try to arrange a car from another wedding car firm we trust;
  • if we can't provide any car, we'll refund everything you've paid for that car.

If we find out before the day, we'll tell you straight away, and you can choose between the replacement and a full refund for that car.

8. If you cancel

Please tell us in writing (email, text or WhatsApp is fine). When you book, we turn other couples away for your date, and wedding car dates are hard to fill at short notice, so we charge for cancellation on this scale:

Because your booking is for a fixed date, the 14-day right to cancel that applies to some online and phone purchases doesn't apply to it.

We'd recommend wedding insurance, which can cover cancellation for reasons outside your control.

9. If we have to cancel

We will only cancel your booking if we really have to, for example if a serious accident, severe weather or a road closure makes it impossible to provide any car. If that happens, we'll tell you as soon as we can, try to arrange another car as in section 7, and if we can't, refund everything you've paid us.

If we can't provide your booking because of something that is our fault, we'll refund everything you've paid us.

  • Confetti outside the car, please, not inside.
  • Seatbelts. Where seatbelts are fitted, everyone must wear them. Which cars have no seatbelts in the back, and can child seats be fitted in any of them?
  • Children must travel with an adult who looks after them.
  • Behaviour. Our chauffeurs are there to look after you. If any passenger is abusive or threatening, or puts the car or anyone in it at risk, the chauffeur can end the journey, and no refund will be due.

  • Belongings. Our chauffeur will check the car, but please take everything with you. If something is left behind, ring us and we'll keep it safe for you to collect.

We keep your name, contact details, wedding date, addresses and booking details so we can provide your cars and keep our accounts. We don't sell your details or pass them to anyone else, except where we have to by law (for example, to HMRC).

Enquiries sent through our website form are delivered to our email by a service called Web3Forms. If you don't go ahead with a booking, we delete your enquiry within 12 months. Booking and payment records are kept for as long as the tax rules require, usually six years. You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at any time.

We'll do everything we reasonably can to get you to your ceremony on time, but we can't control traffic, accidents or road closures. If something goes wrong that is our fault, our responsibility is limited to what you paid us for the booking. This doesn't limit our responsibility for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or anything else the law doesn't allow us to limit.
